Acceptable Use

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Noctia can act in the real world on your behalf, so we hold it, and you, to a short list of rules.

Do not use Noctia to

Message fans who have opted out, or buyers from events you did not run. Sell tickets for events that do not exist or that you are not authorised to sell. Publish misleading line-ups, fake reviews or deceptive urgency. Run ads that break platform or local rules on age, alcohol or venues. Scrape or import audience data you do not have the right to use.

What happens if you do

We may pause the agent, suspend the account or remove published content. Serious or repeated violations end the account without refund.

Reporting

If you believe an event run on Noctia is breaking these rules, write to hello@noctia.com.
