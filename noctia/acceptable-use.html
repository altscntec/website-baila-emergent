Acceptable Use

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Noctia can act in the real world on your behalf, so we hold it, and you, to a short list of rules.

Do not use Noctia to

Send unsolicited bulk email that breaks anti-spam law. Impersonate people or organisations. Sell counterfeit, illegal or regulated goods without the required licences. Publish misleading reviews, fake testimonials or deceptive advertising. Collect personal data without a lawful basis. Attack, probe or overload other systems.

What happens if you do

We may pause the agent, suspend the account or remove published content. Serious or repeated violations end the account without refund.

Reporting

If you believe a company run on Noctia is breaking these rules, write to support@noctia.com.
