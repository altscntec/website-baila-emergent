About Noctia

Written by the founder

We run Latin parties across the Netherlands. Reggaeton, dembow, salsa and bachata, in six cities, for 25,000 fans. Every show has the same rhythm: an on-sale, weeks of ads and posts, a mailbox full of questions about dress code and ID, a night at the door, and a morning spent working out what sold and why.

Noctia is the system we built to do that rhythm for us. It forecasts how a show will sell, runs the ads and moves the money toward what works, keeps every buyer in one database, and handles the night-of details. Then it writes us a note in the morning.

Why it is only for events

General-purpose AI tools know nothing about on-sale waves, price tiers, scan rates or why a Rotterdam show sells faster when the on-sale lands on a Tuesday. Noctia is trained on exactly that, so its forecasts and budget moves are grounded in how tickets actually sell.

What you get

Connect your ticketing and your ad accounts, add the next event, and read the report in the morning. You own everything it builds and every row in your database.

Where it is weak

It makes mistakes. A budget move or a reply can be wrong. High-stakes calls, like a venue change or a refund policy, still deserve a person. Start it on the boring work and widen the leash as it earns it.

Everything it does is logged on the event dashboard, so you see the work and not just the summary.
