Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What we collect

Your account details, the events and instructions you give Noctia, the output it produces, ticket buyer data from the ticketing platforms you connect, activity in the ad and messaging accounts you authorise, and standard technical logs.

How we use it

To forecast, market and run your events, to improve the product, to keep the service secure and to contact you about your account. We do not sell personal data. Fan data is used only for your own events.

Fan data

Ticket buyers in your database are your customers. You are the controller of that data and Noctia processes it on your behalf. Messages to fans go out under your brand and respect their opt-outs.

Subprocessors

We rely on hosting, model, email, messaging and advertising providers to deliver the service. A current list is available on request.

Your rights

You can export or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@noctia.com.
